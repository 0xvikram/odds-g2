Privacy

What Odds keeps and sends

Odds has no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. Last updated 25 November 2026.

Kept on your device

  • The markets you follow.
  • Your paper bets and their prices.
  • Your alert line.

These live in the app's local storage (on the site, your browser's). Unless you link Telegram alerts, they never reach our servers. Clearing site data removes them.

Only if you link Telegram alerts

The glasses run one app at a time, so Odds can't watch markets in the background. If you say “connect Telegram” and send the code to our bot, the Odds backend keeps:

  • a random id for your app (not tied to your name or glasses),
  • your Telegram chat id,
  • the markets you follow, your alert line and your open paper bets,
  • the price at the last alert for each market, so one move alerts you once.

These sit in an Upstash Redis database. Every few minutes the backend checks your markets on Polymarket and sends you a Telegram message when one moves past your line or a paper bet settles. Say “disconnect Telegram” on the glasses, or send /stop to the bot, and all of it is deleted.

Sent while you use it

  • Voice clips, only while you hold to talk, to the Odds backend, which forwards them to Groq for speech-to-text. We don't store the clip or the text.
  • Market searches and ids to the Odds backend, which asks Polymarket's public API.
  • Market titles to the Odds backend, which searches Google News for headlines.

Telegram alerts are sent through Telegram's Bot API, so Telegram sees the messages. The backend runs on Vercel, which keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for a short period. Groq, Polymarket and Google receive requests from our backend, not from your device.
