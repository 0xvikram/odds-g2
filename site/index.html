Why did it move?

The headline behind the jump, right under the price.

When a market moves past your alert line, Odds finds the latest headline that names it. Or just ask “why did it move?”.

  • Real headlines from the last 24 hours, with the outlet and how long ago.
  • No AI writes or picks the line. If nothing matches, it says “No news in 24h”.
  • Your alert line: “alert at 3 points”.
Alerts, even when Odds is closed

G2 shows one app at a time. The move still finds you.

Say “connect Telegram” and tap the link on your phone. Odds checks the markets you follow every few minutes and messages you when one moves past your line, or when a paper bet settles.

  • Opens where it matters: the next time you open Odds, it starts on the market that moved.
  • Settled bets: won or lost, with the payout, the moment a market resolves.
  • Yours to stop: “disconnect Telegram” or /stop deletes everything stored.

Who it's for, and why it's safe

Built for the glance. Careful with the money.

Who it's for

People who already follow prediction markets and can't keep a phone out.

  • Every day: a handful of live questions (a rate decision, a match, an election night, a crypto price), checked between meetings, on a run, on a call.
  • When it matters: the hour a market moves. The alert finds you; the glasses show the price, the trend and the headline.
  • What would pay for it: free to follow and paper-trade; a Pro plan for unlimited alerts, a line per market and a morning brief. Not built yet.

A voice can't spend your money

Voice is great for “show me” and risky for “do it”, so the doing stays on paper.

  • Paper only: no wallet, no keys, no real orders.
  • Rules, not a model, decide: amounts and outcomes come from your words by fixed rules. No exact match, no bet.
  • One tap to confirm, 10 seconds to undo: every bet shows stake, outcome, price and payout first.
  • Silence is never sent: empty presses are dropped before speech-to-text, so nothing is invented.
