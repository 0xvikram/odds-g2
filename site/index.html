How it works

Hold. Say it. Glance.

Everything runs from the ring or the temple touchpad. Hold anywhere to speak; tap to open or confirm; swipe to move between markets; double-tap to go back.

Why did it move?

The headline behind the jump, right under the price.

When a market moves past your alert line in an hour, Odds finds the latest headline that names it and puts it on the card. Or just ask “why did it move?”.

  • Real headlines from the last 24 hours, with the outlet and how long ago.
  • No AI writes or picks the line. If nothing matches, it says “No news in 24h”.
  • Alert line is yours: “alert at 3 points”.
What's inside

Built for a 576 × 288 screen and one finger.

“ ”

Open-vocabulary voice

Hold, speak naturally, let go. “What's bitcoin doing?”, “follow the Fed decision in December”, “put ten bucks on Arsenal”. Speech-to-text runs on our backend; a fixed rule set turns the words into an action.

↕

Your watchlist

Follow up to 20 events. Each row shows the leader, its price and the 1-hour change.

$

Paper portfolio

Bets at the live price, live P&L, “close it” to sell at today's price. When a market resolves, the bet settles itself: WON pays $1 a share, LOST pays nothing.

!

Move alerts, even when Odds is closed

G2 shows one app at a time. Say “connect Telegram” and a big move on a market you follow, or a settled bet, reaches your phone. Next time you open Odds, it starts on that market.

✓

Clean data

Resolved markets and unpriced placeholder slots are filtered out, so the leader is always a real outcome.

Who it's for

People who already watch the odds, and can't keep a phone out.

Every day

Traders and fans of prediction markets who follow a handful of live questions: a rate decision, a match, an election night, a crypto price. Odds is the glance between meetings, on a run, on a call.

When it matters most

The hour a market moves. The Telegram alert finds you wherever you are; the glasses then show the price, the trend and the headline without picking up a phone.

What people would pay for

Free: follow markets, paper bets, a few alerts a day. Pro, a few dollars a month: unlimited alerts, a line per market and a morning brief. Later, real orders with the same tap to confirm. None of the paid plan is built yet.

Safety

A voice can't spend your money.

Voice is great for “show me” and dangerous for “do it”. So Odds keeps the doing on paper, and makes every step visible.

✓

Paper only

No wallet, no keys, no real orders. Bets are a ledger in the glasses app's local storage.

✓

Rules, not a model, decide

Amounts and outcomes come from your words by fixed rules. If it can't match an outcome exactly, it asks again instead of guessing.

✓

One tap to confirm

Every bet shows stake, outcome, price and payout, then waits for “Yes, place it”.

✓

Silence is never sent

Speech models invent words from silence. Odds checks the clip's loudness first and drops empty presses.
