A one-minute tour

  1. Tap a row on the watchlist to open it.
  2. Swipe ↑ ↓ to move between markets.
  3. Open the Fed decision, say “why did it move”.
  4. Say “20 on no change”, then Tap “Yes, place it”.
  5. Double-tap to go back to the watchlist.
